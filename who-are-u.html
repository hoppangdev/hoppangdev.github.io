A NEW HOPPANG PROJECT

Who Are U!

Same faces.
Different intentions.

A supermarket full of ordinary movements. One AI is trying a little too hard to blend in. Can you spot it?

● In development · local prototype

This is an early project introduction. Public play and online multiplayer are not available yet.

01 / THE IDEA

Walk. Watch. Decide.

Who Are U! is a 3D observation game. The clue is in the way someone moves, not in the way they look.

01

Walk the aisles

Explore a 3D supermarket in third person. Shelves and corners change what you can see.

02

Watch the crowd

Everyone is a white mannequin. Look for patterns in movement, pauses and gestures.

03

Choose a back number

Read the number on a character’s back and make your pick before the reveal.

02 / IN PROGRESS

Where the project is now

The first local prototype connects movement, observation, selection, answer reveal, scoring and replay. Korean and English are being supported from the start.

Online multiplayer, public distribution and further polish are planned for later development. No release date has been announced.
