Bathtub Blastoff

A curious bathtub.
A big adventure.

Slide a bathtub off a bathhouse roof and blast off on steam! Boost, ride geysers and skip across water through eight stages, all the way to the Moon.

● Coming soon to web game portals

Controls & support ↗Privacy policyTerms

01 / HOW IT WORKS

One finger, one big flight

01

Time the launch

Tap when the needle hits pink for a PERFECT start and shoot off the bathhouse roof.

02

Fly and boost

Hold anywhere to fire the steam boiler behind the tub, and let go to save steam. Ride geysers and skip across water.

03

Upgrade and go farther

Spend your coins in the workshop on parts that make the next flight longer, then take on the next stage.

02 / SCREENS

A look at the game

Screens from the game. The in-game text is shown in English.

On phones and desktop

Plays in portrait on phones and in landscape on desktop. One touch, click or Space bar is all it takes.

03 / FEATURES

What's inside

  • Eight stages to the Moon

    From Bathhouse Town to the Moon Spa, each with its own ground, gadgets, traps and music. Clear a stage to unlock its Endless mode. Two bonus stages are waiting too.

  • Steam booster

    Hold anywhere to fire the boiler and push the tub forward. Let go to save steam for later.

  • Ten riders with abilities

    A clawfoot tub, a sauna barrel, a rubber duck, a laundry basket and more. Each flies differently and has its own quick-tap ability and parts.

  • Parts and upgrades workshop

    Buy parts that bolt onto your tub where you can see them, and watch your flights get longer.

  • Story in comic panels

    The old bathhouse has run dry! Fly to the Legendary Spring on the Moon and make a new friend at every stop.

  • Daily Challenge and Endless modes

    A Daily Challenge with a twist, Endless mode on cleared stages, and medals and records to chase.

  • 12 languages

    English, Korean, Spanish, French, Italian, German, Turkish, Japanese, Brazilian Portuguese, Russian, Polish and Indonesian.

  • Free, no login

    Open it and play. No account needed.

  • Phone and desktop

    Quick runs, big launches and "just one more" every time, on phones or desktop browsers.
