Common questions

QThere’s no sound

Browsers don’t let a page play sound until you interact with it, so the music and effects start after your first tap or click. If it’s still quiet, check that the game isn’t muted — the Sound button on the title screen, or the M key — then the Sound effects and Music volumes in the Bathhouse Board settings, and your device’s volume.

QHow do I switch between English and Korean?

The first time the game runs, it starts in Korean if your browser is set to Korean, and in English otherwise. You can switch at any time with the EN / 한국어 button on the title screen, or under Language in the Bathhouse Board settings.

QMy progress is gone

Progress is saved in your browser, on this device, separately for each site you play on. So it doesn’t carry over to another browser, device or website — unless that site saves it to your account there. It also disappears when the site data is cleared, after “Reset progress”, or when you close a private window that blocks storage.

We don’t keep a copy of anyone’s save, so we can’t restore it. Sorry!

QHow do I start over?

In the Bathhouse Board, go to Settings and choose “Reset progress”. (The board opens from the Board button on the title screen — it appears after your first flight — or from the board icon in the workshop.) This erases your coins, parts, stages, trophies and tickets on that device and keeps your language and volume settings.

QDo I have to pay or sign up?

No. The game is free and has no accounts. Ads are shown by the site where you play, and rewarded ads only play when you press their button. If a platform offers optional purchases, you never need them to progress.

QThe game is stuck on the loading screen

Reload the page. If it keeps happening, a browser extension such as an ad blocker may be blocking a game file: allow the site, or try another browser. If that doesn’t help, please report it.

Report a bug

Email hoppangdev@gmail.com and include:

  1. Device — for example “Galaxy S25” or “Windows laptop”
  2. Browser — Chrome, Safari, Samsung Internet…
  3. Where you played — the site or app, such as Poki, CrazyGames, Playgama or YouTube
  4. Game version — at the bottom of the title screen, for example “v0.3.0”
  5. What happened — and what you were doing just before
  6. A screenshot, if you can
