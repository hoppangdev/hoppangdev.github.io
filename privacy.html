Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-06

Bathtub Blastoff (Korean title: 날아라 욕조!) is a free browser game made by Hoppang, an independent developer — “the developer”, “we” or “us” on this page. This policy explains what happens to information when you play it.

The short version

  • There are no accounts: no sign-up, no login, no chat.
  • Your progress and settings are saved in your own browser, on your own device.
  • The game itself sends nothing to us. It has no analytics, no cookies and no network requests of its own.
  • The site where you play (for example Poki, CrazyGames or Playgama) runs its own ads, statistics and accounts under its own privacy policy.

1What the game saves on your device

The game saves your progress and settings — coins, upgrades, records and medals, collected items, language, volume and accessibility options — in your browser’s local storage, on your own device. All of its keys start with bathtub-blastoff/. The main ones are bathtub-blastoff/v1 (your progress) and bathtub-blastoff/meta (the Bathhouse Board: missions, quests, trophies, wardrobe and settings; bathtub-blastoff/meta.bak is its backup copy). Small ones hold the shop and offer counters (bathtub-blastoff/shop), the capsule machine (bathtub-blastoff/capsule), your records and medals (bathtub-blastoff/compete), the tips you have already seen (bathtub-blastoff/hints), small screen choices such as the Workshop tab (bathtub-blastoff/ui) and the graphics quality (bathtub-blastoff/gfx). On sites that keep your save in your account there, the game may also keep a couple of bookkeeping entries with the same prefix (a marker that your save was moved, and a backup of a save it could not read). A copy of the game that is not played through a portal, for example one hosted on the developer’s own site, also keeps one key with a different prefix, hoppang/splash-day: the date the studio intro last played, so that it is shown at most once a day. This data stays in your browser: the game doesn’t send it to us, and we never see it.

If your browser blocks storage (some private or incognito modes do), the game still works, but your progress only lasts for that session.

Where a portal offers cloud saves (for example Playgama, YouTube Playables or a CrazyGames account), your progress may be stored by that portal under its own policy.

2What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect personal data.

  • The game has no account system: no sign-up, no login, no profile, no chat and no user-generated content.
  • It makes no network requests of its own. All of its pictures and music are generated in code, on your device.
  • It sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics of its own.

The only way we receive anything about you is if you choose to email us — see If you email us.

3The sites where you play

Bathtub Blastoff is distributed through game portals and platforms such as Poki, CrazyGames and Playgama. Playgama can also bring the game to YouTube Playables, and other platforms, such as Toss, may follow. Each of them is run by another company.

Advertising, analytics and statistics, accounts, cloud saves and any payments are provided by these partners under their own terms and privacy policies — not by us. To see what they collect and why, please read the privacy policy of the site where you play:

  • Poki — poki.com/en/privacy (Privacy Center)
  • CrazyGames — crazygames.com/privacy-policy
  • Playgama — playgama.com/confidential
  • YouTube Playables — Google Privacy Policy

4Ads and purchases

Ads are shown by the portal or platform, not by us. Rewarded ads are always optional: one only plays after you press the button that offers it.

If a platform offers optional in-game purchases (such as gold tickets), that platform processes the payment, and we never see your payment details. You never need to spend real money to progress in the game.

5Children

The game is generally family-friendly: cartoon slapstick, no blood, no real-money gambling and no chat. The game does have a capsule machine with random prizes (its odds are shown in the machine), but its tokens are free and can never be bought. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Ads and data handling on each portal follow that portal’s own child-safety and privacy policies (for example, age-appropriate ads). Parents and guardians: please check the policy of the site where your child plays. If you think a child has sent us personal information by email, let us know and we will delete it.

6If you email us

When you write to contact@hoppangdev.shop, we receive your email address and whatever you put in the message, such as screenshots or details about your device. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems in the game. We don’t sell it or share it, and we delete it once it’s no longer needed for that. You can ask us to delete your messages at any time.

7Deleting your data

  • In the game: open the Bathhouse Board, go to Settings and choose “Reset progress”. This erases your coins, parts, stages, trophies and tickets on that device; your language and volume settings stay as they are.
  • In your browser: clear the site data (cookies and other site data) for the site where you play. This removes what the game saved in that browser. It also removes that site’s other data, so you may be signed out of the portal.
  • Cloud saves: if a portal keeps your progress in your account there, delete it through that portal.

We don’t have a copy of your game data, so there is nothing to delete on our side — apart from any emails you sent us.

8Changes to this policy

If the game changes in a way that affects your data — for example a new feature that sends information anywhere — we will update this page and the date at the top before that change goes live.

9Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: contact@hoppangdev.shop
